How It Works?

The system tailors recommendations to each individual’s Dosha (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), ensuring that the food choices support their specific health needs.

Learn more

  • Assessment
    Users input their food choices or ingredients

  • Analysis
    The system grades the food based on Ayurvedic criteria such as
    taste (Rasa), qualities (Guna), potency (Virya),
    post-digestive effect (Vipaka), and impact on Doshas.

  • Results
    The user receives a grading report highlighting the food’s alignment
    with their Ayurvedic body type and suggestions for balance.

Know Your Dosha

Discover the unique blueprint of your mind-body constitution by taking our quick and insightful Dosha quiz. In Ayurveda, your Dosha is a fundamental element that defines your physical, mental, and emotional tendencies. It governs everything from how you digest food to how you respond to stress and the environment around you. By understanding your Dosha—whether it’s Vata, Pitta, or Kapha—you gain valuable insights into how to maintain harmony and balance in your life. This knowledge can guide you in making mindful lifestyle and dietary choices tailored to your individual needs, helping you achieve optimal health and well-being. Take the quiz now to begin your journey toward a more balanced, energized, and fulfilling life.
